Reverb

A retro music player for iPhone.

Spin the wheel, or turn the knob of a hi-fi deck. Full songs play through Apple Music, 30-second previews work without it, and your own songs play too.

Coming soon to the App Store

Spin to scroll

A wheel under your thumb, a soft click on every step, and menus that are quick to learn.

Two retro looks

A pocket player with a wheel, or, with Reverb Pro, a hi-fi deck with a glowing display and a big knob. Then pick the finish you like.

Your own music

Import songs from the Files app, AirDrop or your computer. Reverb finds the album art, or use your own photo as the cover.

Private by design

No ads, no tracking, no accounts. Your library syncs through your own iCloud.
